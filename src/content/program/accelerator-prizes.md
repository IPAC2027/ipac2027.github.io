## IEEE NPSS PAST Awards

The 2027 awards of the IEEE Nuclear and Plasma Sciences Society (NPSS) Particle Accelerator Science and Technology (PAST) Technical Committee will be presented at IPAC'27.

:::grid{columns=2}
:::card{icon="🏆" title="PAST Award"}
For outstanding contributions to the development of particle accelerator science and technology. At least one award will be given to an individual early in their career.
* Prize: 3,000 USD and an IEEE NPSS plaque
* Contact: Wolfram Fischer (BNL), [wfischer@bnl.gov](mailto:wfischer@bnl.gov)
:::

:::card{icon="🎓" title="PAST Doctoral Student Award"}
For significant and innovative contributions to particle accelerator science and technology in a doctoral thesis defended no more than 24 months before the nomination deadline.
* Prize: 2,000 USD and an IEEE NPSS plaque
* Contact: Fulvia Pilat (ORNL), [pilatfc@ornl.gov](mailto:pilatfc@ornl.gov)
:::
:::

:::highlight
**Call for nominations:** nominations for both awards are due by **31 March 2027**.  For more information, please visit the [IEEE NPSS PAST site](https://ieee-npss.org/technical-committees/particle-accelerator-science-and-technology/).
:::
